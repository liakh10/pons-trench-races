01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Pons Trench Races

A toy kart race in the browser. Eight drivers, one pastel circuit with mint neon on the walls, three laps, and a podium that only ever fits one ego at a time.

The whole thing runs on a single page with Three.js. Every kart body is the Pons logo, the glossy letter P, laid flat with the driver sitting in the notch. Billboards, flags and the blimp along the track carry Pons and Robinhood Chain marks because that is where the token lives.

ENGINE
Three.js 0.171 in one index.html, no build step, no framework
PLATFORM
Desktop and mobile browsers with WebGL
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token launched through Pons
RACE LENGTH
3 laps, roughly one minute against the field

04 · RULESRace rules

Five red lights, then GO. The grid starts a few metres behind the line, so the first crossing is lap one, not a free lap.

race ├── laps 3, position and lap counter live top right ├── top speed 40 units per second on the road ├── boost pads 4 glowing mint pads, +32% speed for 1.3 seconds, only on the middle of the road ├── off the road top speed halves and the kart slows fast, get back on the grey ├── walls the mint neon walls stop you and eat some speed, sound effect included ├── contact karts push each other sideways and trade a little speed └── the field the AI runs close to your pace and closes in when you lead, nobody gets to cruise

05 · PODIUMResults

The race ends when you cross the line on lap three. The others finish on their own and the table fills in as they arrive.

POSITION
Ranked by who finished first, then by distance for anyone still out there
TIMES
Seconds from GO to the finish line, shown for every driver who made it
THE LINE
The winner gets their own quote under the table, so plan for Musk
AGAIN
RACE AGAIN restarts with the same driver, PIT LANE goes back to the grid

06 · SCENERYThe track

One closed circuit, twelve corners, white pastel buildings and small round trees. Everything on it is built from primitives in code, nothing is a downloaded model.

trackside ├── start gantry five lights, pons and Robinhood Chain plates on the crossbar ├── jumbotron plays the race clip on loop next to the first corner ├── billboards six of them, pons and Robinhood Chain, alternating sides ├── monuments three chrome Pons P statues, slowly turning ├── flags 28 poles along the walls, Pons P and the Robinhood feather ├── decals logos painted on the asphalt at the start and mid track └── blimp one, circling, P on both sides

07 · TOKEN$PTR token

$PTR launches on Robinhood Chain through the Pons launchpad. The contract address lives on the pit board in the game and right here. Until launch both say SOON.

08 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

There is no backend, no account and no leaderboard. The site does not write anything to your browser either. Close the tab and the race never happened, which some drivers prefer.

SOUND
The site opens straight on the grid. Sound starts with the SOUND ON prompt or your first tap anywhere, the button in the corner mutes music and effects together
MUSIC
One CC0 track, so it can play here without a credit line
REDUCED MOTION
If your system asks for it, the camera stops zooming with speed and the smoke stays off

10 · FAQQuestions

Why is my kart shaped like a PBecause it is the Pons logo. Every kart on the grid is the same letter in a different colour, the driver sits in the cut out corner
Can I winYes. Hit the boost pads, stay off the white edges and do not touch the walls. The field stays close on purpose, so a clean last lap decides it
Does it work on a phoneYes. Throttle is automatic, steer by holding a side of the screen. The pit board and the buttons move to the bottom
Where is the chart buttonOff the site until the contract is live. It comes back with the address
Who made the driversThey are toy versions built from spheres and boxes, with faces drawn in code. Nobody was scanned
